Centuries of Celuk metallurgy.

For over a century, the smithing families of Celuk Village have guarded the techniques of Balinese precious metalwork: jawan (micro-granulation), bun (hand-drawn wire filigree), and cold-hammered structural forging.

We reject hollow factory stamping. Every Artfuli piece carries the dense weight of hand-forged metal and the direct signature of the smith who shaped it.

By operating our own private foundry and finishing benches in Sukawati, we eliminate luxury distributor markups while guaranteeing fair artisan remuneration and complete metallurgical traceability.

Radical Material Transparency

Fine jewelry requires scientific proof, not marketing adjectives. Every melt batch in our Celuk foundry undergoes non-destructive X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) analysis prior to hallmarking.

Each acquired piece ships with its printed XRF batch card recording silver fineness, gold micron thickness, and zero-allergen compliance.

92.5% Pure Silver + 7.5% Oxygen-Free Copper

We alloy pure 999 silver bullion exclusively with electrolytic oxygen-free copper in our Sukawati crucibles. This prevents fire-scale porosity and delivers higher tensile hardness than commercial scrap silver.

Verification Protocol: Olympus Vanta XRF Spectrometer · Batch Tolerance +/- 0.05%

Hand-carved wax to molten silver.

Our workshop unites ancestral Balinese hand-forging with vacuum-assisted lost-wax casting, allowing architectural precision without sacrificing the warmth of human touch.

  1. Hand-Carved Wax and Granulation

    Every form begins as a hand-carved jewelers wax master or an arrangement of individually rolled silver spheres (jawan) placed one by one with a fine bamboo nib.

    Bench Stage: Sukawati Carving Studio · 6 to 18 Hours per Master

  2. Vacuum Lost-Wax Investment

    Wax trees are encased in fine gypsum investment, burned out in kiln cycles over 12 hours, and cast with de-oxidized 925 sterling silver to prevent porosity.

    Foundry Stage: 960°C Melt Crucible · Argon Shielded Pour

  3. Agate Burnishing and Assay Stamp

    After hand-filing and triple ultrasonic cleansing, artisans burnish the metal edges with smooth Balinese river agate stones before stamping the AJ crucible hallmark.

    Finishing Stage: Natural River Agate Compression · Final XRF Scan
