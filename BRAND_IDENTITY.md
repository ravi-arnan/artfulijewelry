## 2. Value Proposition
Artfuli Jewelry bridges generations of master silversmithing and goldsmithing from Bali with contemporary architectural minimalism. We craft heirloom-grade 925 sterling silver and thick 18k gold vermeil jewelry backed by laboratory XRF assay verification, offering direct-from-atelier transparency for individual collectors and bespoke partners worldwide.

## 3. Aesthetic Direction
Artfuli Jewelry operates at the intersection of two benchmarks:
1. **Effortless Commerce (Mejuri Inspiration)**: Intuitive product discovery, transparent material disclosures, frictionless variant selection, and everyday wearability.
2. **Editorial Tactility (Kinn Studio Inspiration)**: Warm archival tones, generous negative space, story-driven lookbook layouts, and reverence for physical craftsmanship and heirloom longevity.

## 4. Brand Pillars
### I. Balinese Heritage (Warisan Kriya Bali)
Every piece is shaped, soldered, and polished by master artisans in Bali. We honor traditional Balinese granulation (jawan), filigree (bun), and ancestral silversmithing techniques while integrating modern precision.

### II. Radical Material Transparency
Luxury requires proof. Every batch of precious metal undergoes X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectrometry testing to guarantee certified 925 sterling silver purity and a minimum of 2.5 microns of 18k solid gold layering for vermeil pieces, completely free of nickel and cadmium.

### III. Modern Atelier Access
Beyond ready-to-wear collections, Artfuli opens its workshop doors through digital concierge services: custom 3D CAD modeling, lost-wax precious metal casting, and low-minimum private label production.

## 5. Brand Voice & Copywriting Rules
- **Tone**: Sophisticated, calm, factual, warm, and discerning.
- **Language Standards**: International Editorial English (primary) or High-Editorial Indonesian.
- **Strict Punctuation & Symbol Guardrails**:
  - **Zero Emojis**: Never use emojis in UI labels, notifications, metadata, or body copy.
  - **Zero Em Dashes**: Never use the em dash character. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or separate sentences with periods.
  - **No Hyperbole**: Avoid discount-driven urgency ("BUY NOW!", "LIMITED FLASH SALE!"). Use measured editorial invitations ("Explore the Archive", "Request Atelier Consultation", "Acquire This Piece").

## 6. Visual Identity Assets
- **The Artfuli Wordmark**: High-contrast editorial serif set in uppercase with expanded tracking (`tracking-[0.22em]`).
- **The Artfuli Monogram ("AJ" / Bali Crucible Mark)**: A minimalist geometric interlocking mark inspired by traditional Balinese metal stamps, used on favicons, assay certificates, and navigation seals.
- **Photography Treatment**:
  - Authentic product and process photography curated from the local archive (`old/images/produk/`).
  - Warm daylight color grading, subtle 4:5 portrait framing for product cards, and 16:9 or 3:2 wide framing for Bali workshop narratives.
